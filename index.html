About JKKS Industries

Invented by firefighters who live the problem every day.

JKKS Industries was founded by 4 members of the Paramus Volunteer Fire Department. Between the team we have over 120 years of firefighting experience and two of the members are ex-chiefs.

On average we run almost 1,500 calls a year, so we certainly get enough action to constantly keep us looking for new ways to make the task at hand easier.

We developed the Rice Applicator with the understanding that available manpower continues to diminish in the fire service. Our nozzle can be deployed by a single firefighter.

The Rice Applicator delivers large volumes of water directly on the battery case to prevent thermal runaway. There is no need to lift the vehicle and put firefighters in harm’s way while working to extinguish the fire.

The device is named in honor of Tim Rice, a Hackensack firefighter and Paramus captain who passed away in 2021.
